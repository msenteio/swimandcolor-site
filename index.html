On the aesthetic, cultural & emotional importance of play

Swim and Color is a practice about taking play seriously. It started as a newsletter — part essay, part field notes — on why the things we make for fun carry so much of what we actually mean. It has since become the name for everything under it: the writing, the projects, the workshops, the small experiments that don’t fit anywhere else.

Made by Michelle Senteio, a creative technologist who spent eight years turning cutting-edge visual technology into things people want to play with, and taught at NYU Tisch’s Game Center.
