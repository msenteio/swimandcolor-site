On the aesthetic, cultural & emotional importance of play

Swim and Color makes the case for play as a cultural imperative — not leisure, not time off from real life, but how a culture works out what it values before it can say so out loud.

The things people build for no reason carry a moment’s values earlier, and more honestly, than anything made on purpose. Reading them is the whole project.
